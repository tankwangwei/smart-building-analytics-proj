## What could not be checked

- **Reproduction from the raw 1-minute data (A1, A2 second half):** `combined_by_floor.csv` (618 MB) is not in the repository, so recomputing energy and peaks from raw minutes was not done. Instead the stored KPIs were reconciled against the 15-minute detail (see A2 below).
- **Refitting every method from the raw data (B1):** needs `combined_by_floor.csv`. The notebook exports the Jul 2018 – Jun 2019 baseline hourly demand and the Apr–Jun 2019 validation predictions, so the audit checks that M1 is exactly the baseline's hour-of-week median, that the lag models use only values from >= 24 h / 168 h earlier, and that the error bands are learned on Apr–Jun 2019, never on the test period.
- **Realistic weather (B6):** re-scoring with Open-Meteo's historical-forecast archive needs network access and was not run. The weather models still use observed weather, so their accuracy is optimistic.
- **Eyeball precision/recall (A5):** needs a person. `docs/verification_event_sample.csv` lists 20 random flagged events and 20 random unflagged unit-days (fixed seed) with an empty `judgement` column to fill in.

Coverage near 80% means the band is well calibrated; above means conservative (wide), below means too narrow. With public holidays forecast as zero (the dashboard's default, fair because holidays are known in advance) the band covers about 80–90% on every floor. Without that adjustment Floor 3 drops to about half: its Jul–Dec 2019 closures cause misses larger than anything in Apr–Jun, so a band learned there is too narrow for them.
